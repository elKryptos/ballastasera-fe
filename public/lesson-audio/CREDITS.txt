Samples used by the /impara-a-ballare lesson's band.
All were trimmed (leading silence removed, cut to length with a fade-out),
peak-normalised, mixed down to mono and re-encoded as 64 kbps MP3.

Percussion (conga-*, tumba-*, bongo-*, bell-*, clave, guiro-*, shaker-*, cabasa-*)
and piano (piano-*):
  Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), by Sam Gossner and contributors
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Bass (bass-*), trumpet (trumpet-*), trombone (trombone-*),
electric guitar (guitar-*) and acoustic guitar (acoustic-*):
  tonejs-instruments, by Nicholas Brosowsky
  https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
